# System Rationale and Intended Use

## Overview

This document defines the design intent, constraints, and intended use of the Tamarind Koji-Yam Powder system.

It is provided to clarify the engineering principles behind the fermentation process and to prevent misinterpretation of scope or application.

---

## Purpose

This system describes a reproducible two-stage fermentation method combining:

- fungal fermentation (Aspergillus oryzae)
- lactic acid bacterial fermentation (Lactobacillus plantarum)

applied to starch- and plant-based substrates to produce a stable powdered food ingredient with controlled flavor development.

---

## Design Principles

### 1. Sequential biological transformation
Fermentation stages are separated to isolate enzymatic breakdown (koji) from acid formation (LAB fermentation).

### 2. Whole-substrate processing
Ingredients are processed with minimal fractionation to preserve structural and flavor complexity.

### 3. Dry-state functionality
The system is designed to remain stable and functional after dehydration and milling.

### 4. Food-grade constraint
All inputs and microbial systems are selected from food-safe, widely used fermentation organisms and materials.

---

## Intended Use

This system is intended for:

- food ingredient development
- culinary formulation research
- fermentation process exploration
- flavor system prototyping in food manufacturing

---

## Non-Goals / Out of Scope

This system is not intended for:

- medical or therapeutic applications
- pharmaceutical use
- isolated nutrient extraction systems
- non-food industrial chemical production

---

## Operational Boundaries

This document does not define commercial outcomes, branding, or product positioning. It defines only process logic and intended functional scope.
