# Formulation

## Base Ratio (reference formulation)

- Rice: 40%
- Yam powder: 20%
- Brown sugar: 10%
- Wheat berry: 20%
- Tamarind: 10%

## Variations

### Higher umami
Increase koji substrate ratio

### Higher acidity
Increase tamarind ratio

### Milder profile
Reduce LAB fermentation time (24–36 hours)
