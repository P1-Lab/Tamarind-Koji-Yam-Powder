# Production Process

## Stage 1: Koji Fermentation

### Ingredients
- Arborio rice
- yam powder
- brown sugar
- Aspergillus oryzae

### Method
1. Wash and soak rice (4–8 hours)
2. Steam until cooked but grain-separated
3. Cool to 30–35°C
4. Mix with yam powder + brown sugar
5. Inoculate with koji spores
6. Incubate at 28–30°C, 70–90% humidity
7. Ferment 48–72 hours with periodic mixing

### Endpoint
Full white koji coverage and sweet fermented aroma

---

## Drying + Milling
- Dry at <45°C or freeze-dry
- Grind to fine powder (80–120 microns)

---

## Stage 2: LAB Fermentation

### Ingredients
- koji powder
- cooked wheat berry
- tamarind
- Lactobacillus plantarum

### Method
1. Cook wheat berry and cool
2. Prepare tamarind paste or slurry
3. Combine all components
4. Add sterile water to form thick slurry
5. Inoculate with LAB
6. Ferment at 20–30°C for 24–72 hours
7. Target pH: 3.5–4.0

---

## Final Drying
- Dehydrate <45°C or freeze-dry
- Final moisture <5%
- Grind to uniform powder
